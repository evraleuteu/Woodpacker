# Woodpecker AI
## Vision

Become the world's first AI-powered Mastery Operating System that transforms any learning material into a scientifically structured repetition and recall program.

## Mission

Help learners achieve automatic recall, fluency, and mastery from content they already own.

## Core Insight

People do not need more courses.

People already have:

- Textbooks
- PDFs
- Lecture notes
- Audiobooks
- Podcasts
- Language courses
- Exam preparation books
- YouTube transcripts
- Voice recordings
- Accent training materials

The real problem is:

- Forgetting
- Lack of structured review
- Lack of deliberate repetition

Woodpecker AI transforms existing content into a mastery system.

## Product Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Language Mastery

Transform learning material into:

- Vocabulary Decks
- Grammar Decks
- Reading Decks
- Listening Decks
- Pattern Decks

**Goal:** Understand the language.

### Pillar 2 — Speaking Mastery

Transform every lesson into structured speaking practice.

Generated automatically from:

- textbook dialogues
- grammar lessons
- vocabulary
- exercises
- listening passages

**Goal:** Speak automatically without mentally translating.

**Goal:** Sound natural.
